About Us

and cool homes.

Theo's Airconditioning Services is a local HVAC team in San Fernando, Pampanga — started in May 2026 to give families and businesses aircon service they can rely on.

Our Story

From one technician to your go-to aircon team

Theo saw the same thing again and again around Pampanga: late technicians, unclear prices and units that broke down again a month after "repair".

So in May 2026 he started Theo's Airconditioning Services with a simple promise — show up on time, explain the problem in plain words, charge a fair price and do the job properly. Every unit we touch is cleaned, tested and checked before we leave.

Today we help homeowners, renters, small offices and shops across San Fernando and nearby towns keep cool through the hottest months of the year.
